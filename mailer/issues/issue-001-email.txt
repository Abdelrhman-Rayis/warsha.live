AI in the Khaleej Classroom. Issue #001
What Gulf faculty should do about AI this semester

Hi {{first_name}},

Most university AI debates have focused on student ChatGPT use. That debate
is still alive. But the bigger shift is already here: AI is moving from
chatbot to workflow. OpenAI is pushing campus-wide ChatGPT Edu and shared
workspace agents for repeatable work, Anthropic is building tutor-style
Learning mode into Claude Projects, and Google is making NotebookLM and
Gems part of education workflows.

That means Gulf faculty are no longer only deciding whether students may
use AI. They are deciding where AI belongs in courses, research supervision,
and institutional processes.

PEDAGOGY PATTERN
Build one course-specific assistant that knows your syllabus, rubric,
readings, and sample feedback, then use it to help students think without
writing the assignment for them. OpenAI explicitly supports course-specific
GPTs in university workspaces, and Anthropic's Projects plus Learning mode
show the same bounded-course pattern from a different angle.

TOOL WORTH YOUR TIME
NotebookLM is grounded in your own sources, returns inline citations, and
now supports Arabic output. The real limits are source quality, OCR, and
admin enablement.

GOVERNANCE ACTION
If a tool can act across university systems, treat it as governance, not
just productivity. OpenAI's workspace agents can work across tools and ask
for approvals on sensitive actions; UAE and Saudi public frameworks also
expect higher-risk and AI-driven processing to be reviewed seriously.

PROMPT TO STEAL THIS WEEK
"You are a study partner for [COURSE NAME]. Use only the uploaded syllabus,
readings, rubric, and feedback examples. Never write the assignment. Ask
the next useful question instead."
